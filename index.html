Med Supplies

Endosurgery

Pain & Rehab

Critical Care

Ultrasound

Enviro

Autoclave sterilization units, hospital medical waste treatment, cleanroom air purifiers, and environmental controls.

Advance Solutions For Healthcare

SynergyMed is a premier medical device company based in Jakarta, committed to providing advanced medical technologies, excellence in service, and competitive solutions across Indonesia.

Our Vision

To be a trusted leader in medical technology, empowering healthcare providers with innovative, reliable, and sustainable solutions that improve patient outcomes and advance nation healthcare.

Our Mission

  • Deliver high-quality, clinically relevant medical devices across our product business units. Medical Imaging, Critical Care, Ultrasound (USG), Ortho & Neuro, Endosurgery, Med Supplies, and Enviro.
  • Foster long-term partnerships with healthcare professionals, institutions, and global technology leaders to bring world class innovations to the market.
  • Uphold the highest standards of integrity, safety, and regulatory compliance in every product and service we provide.
  • Invest in our people, empowering a multidisciplinary team to deliver excellence, responsive support, and customer-focused solutions.
  • Promote sustainability and social responsibility by integrating environmentally friendly practices into our operations and offerings.

Our Value

Integrity Upholding honesty, transparency, and ethics in every aspect of our business.
Excellence Committed to the highest quality in our products, services, and customer support.
Innovation Delivering new technologies and solutions relevant to clinical needs and healthcare advancements.
Customer Focus Putting patient safety and user needs at the heart of everything we do.
Collaboration Building strong partnerships with healthcare professionals, global partners, and our team.
Reliability Providing consistent, safe, and dependable products and services.
Sustainability Contributing to environmental sustainability through responsible solutions and business practices.
